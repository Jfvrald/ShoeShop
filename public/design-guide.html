01 / THE POINT OF VIEW

FOR THE CULTURE.

A reconstruction inspired by the supplied Sole Rise references. Editorial energy meets a straightforward, usable marketplace.

01 — PRODUCT FIRST

Let the pair do the talking.

Recognizable shoes, clean product stages, and expressive scale. Keep names, condition, seller, and pricing easy to find.

02 — PEOPLE ALWAYS

Make room for everyone.

Buyer, seller, or reseller. Give each person a clear next step and a community where they can find their people.

03 — CLARITY OVER HYPE

Keep the promise honest.

Specific copy. Real local state. No invented ratings or social proof. Demo actions are explicitly identified.

05 / THREE WAYS TO BELONG

ONE PLATFORM.
YOUR NEXT MOVE.

A single local profile can switch roles. All flows work in this browser; they create no real accounts, payments, or public listings.

01 / Buyer

  1. Discover and filter the collection.
  2. Check condition and select a US size.
  3. Add to bag and adjust quantity.
  4. Complete a clearly labeled demo checkout.
  5. Review the saved local order.

Buying remains available without a profile.

02 / Seller

  1. Create a Seller profile.
  2. Open your seller studio.
  3. Set name, price, stock, condition, and sizes.
  4. Publish to the local collection.
  5. Restock or remove your listing.

Inventory metrics come from the actual demo data.

03 / Reseller

  1. Choose the Reseller role.
  2. Build your own curated inventory.
  3. Describe the pair as New or Pre-loved.
  4. Set a price and available sizes.
  5. Join the second sole community circle.

Reseller listings default to Pre-loved condition.

06 / BUILT TO MOVE WITH YOU

THE SAME SOUL.
EVERY SCREEN.

Adapt hierarchy and spacing, not just scale. Keep shopping tasks predictable on a phone, tablet, or desktop.

DESKTOP

Above 1100px · 5% side gutters

Four product columns. Split hero and community. Three marketplace pathways. Horizontal navigation.

TABLET

761–1100px · Reduced gaps

Compact product grid and navigation. Simplified footer. Product metadata wraps to preserve readable prices.

MOBILE

760px and below · 20px side gutters

Two product columns. Stacked editorial sections. Mobile menu, full-width bag, and single-column product details.

Review targets: 320, 390, 768, 1024, and 1440px, plus 200% zoom. Real browser visual verification is pending in this session.

07 / DEPTH WITHOUT DISTRACTION

FEEL THE MOVEMENT.
KEEP THE CONTROL.

Subtle parallax enhances the opening scene. The interface stays immediate, readable, and usable without animation.

SCROLL

Two layers. A little depth.

The shoe and decorative lettering move at different rates. Movement is bounded and scheduled with animation frames.

PREFERENCE

Respect reduced motion.

Turn off parallax, smooth scroll, transitions, and image zoom when the user requests reduced motion.

INTERACTION

Keep every action reachable.

Named controls, visible focus, semantic headings, labeled inputs, a skip link, and native dialogs with Escape dismissal.
